Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 27, 2026

What This Site Does

ISS Planet Transit Tool (issplanet.com) is a free tool that predicts when the International Space Station passes in front of planets as seen from your location. All calculations run in your browser — no data is sent to our servers.

Location Data

If you click "Detect My Location," your browser asks for permission to share your coordinates. This data is used only in your browser to compute ISS passes and transit paths. Your location is never stored or transmitted to us. You can also enter coordinates manually.

Google APIs

This site uses the Google Maps JavaScript API to fetch terrain elevation data when you click the transit path map. These requests are sent directly from your browser to Google. Google's use of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the tool (e.g., page views, general geographic region). Google Analytics uses cookies. You can opt out by using a browser extension such as Google Analytics Opt-out or by enabling "Do Not Track" in your browser settings.

Cookies

This site does not set any cookies of its own. Google Analytics may set cookies as described above.

Third-Party Services

This site loads resources from the following third-party services:

  • Google Maps / Google Analytics (Google LLC)
  • CelesTrak (satellite tracking data)
  • OpenStreetMap / Esri (map tiles)
  • jsDelivr / unpkg (open-source libraries)

Each service has its own privacy policy governing how it handles requests from your browser.

Data We Collect

None. This site has no database, no user accounts, and no server-side processing. All computation happens in your browser.
