Safety

This tool calculates geographic locations where ISS planet transits may be visible. These locations may be on private property, in restricted areas, near hazards, or otherwise unsafe or inaccessible. It is your responsibility to:

  • Respect private property and obtain permission before entering.
  • Obey all local laws, regulations, and posted signs.
  • Stay away from roads, railways, cliffs, bodies of water, and other hazards.
  • Be aware of your surroundings, especially at night.
  • Never trespass, block traffic, or put yourself or others at risk.

Accuracy

Transit paths, times, and magnitudes are estimates based on orbital prediction data (TLE) and elevation models. These predictions are not guaranteed to be accurate. Orbital data degrades over time, and actual conditions may differ from predictions. Always refresh ISS data close to the event for the best results.
